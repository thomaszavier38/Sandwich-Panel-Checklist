Disclaimer and limitations

This checklist records Kingspan’s visual observations of the installation as it stood at the time of the site visit. Kingspan personnel were not present during the installation works undertaken by the installer site team, and therefore this report does not verify or confirm how the works were carried out.

The site visit was limited to areas that were visible and safely accessible at the time of the visit. No opening-up, dismantling, measurements, calculations, intrusive investigations, performance testing or other verification works were undertaken. Concealed conditions, supporting construction, fixing capacities, panel spans and loads, fire stopping and the completed system performance were not verified.

The absence of any comment or observation does not indicate that the relevant work or item is compliant, acceptable, complete or free from defects. This checklist is based solely on visual observations and does not constitute design advice, engineering calculations, a Professional Engineer’s endorsement, certification, approval, acceptance, confirmation of completion or warranty approval.

It must not be relied upon by any party to certify the works undertaken on site or for handover, work-completion or acceptance purposes. Responsibility for the design, installation, rectification, compliance and acceptance of the work remains with the installer, main contractor and the relevant project consultants and other parties responsible for the work.

Accordingly, this checklist should be viewed solely as a record of Kingspan’s observations at the time of the site visit and should not be construed as confirmation of the adequacy, compliance, completion or performance of the installation.
